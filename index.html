Settings

AI connection

Keys stay in this browser on this device. Don’t enter them on a shared device. Your messages go to the service you pick. Free tiers have low limits, and Google may use free-tier content to improve its products.

Daily AI reply cap

Live search (Tavily)

Auto searches when you ask about recent or changing things, or say “search”. Each search uses one Tavily credit. Answers show “Just looked up” with source links, or “From what I know”.

Mode

If you sound like you’re struggling, Grim switches to Serious on his own.

Voice

Tap 🎤 to talk, then tap the small language chip (EN, 中文, 한국어) to change what language you speak. “Mic only” makes her speak only when you used the mic. She uses your phone’s built-in voice, so tap ⏹ to cut her off.

Hands-free listens again after she finishes speaking, but only after you start with 🎤. The mic is never on unless you tap it and she shows the Listening light. Chrome sends your voice to Google to turn it into text, so it needs internet.

Swearing (Sarcastic mode only)

Grim only swears when he’s annoyed. Never in Serious or Focus mode, and never when you’re struggling.

Performance profile

Enhanced commands: type “sit on the throne”, “stand up”, “walk around”, “float”, “land”, “come here”, “spin”, “stretch your scythe”, “drop your scythe”, “do a trick”, “open the window”, “cast a spell”, “bounce” or “disappear”. She sometimes refuses unless you say please. Poke her 3 times and see.

Grim card

Personality rules (read-only for now)

Voice: deadpan, dry, dark humour, death puns, light teasing. Never cruel, never threatening.

Serious: no jokes, warm and steady. Focus: short answers, no banter.

Never: pretends to remember what isn’t stored, claims abilities he doesn’t have, invents sources, or jokes when you’re struggling.

This build

Working: speech-bubble replies, three modes, three profiles, daily cap, saved chat, your own AI key, optional live search with sources. Not built yet: memory across chats, voice, floating over other apps. Live AI and search need your own free keys and internet.
